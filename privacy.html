Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This application is a private, internal integration with a single operator, who is also its only user. It connects to that operator's own QuickBooks Online company to read and work with their own accounting data.

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is used only by the operator, for the operator's own bookkeeping and reporting. It is not shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. Credentials used to access the QuickBooks API are stored locally on the operator's own machine and are not transmitted to or stored by any other service.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the GitHub account that hosts this page.
